Vexomiser: INDIGENA Integration — Consolidated Results

We replace the information-content (IC) phenotype similarity inside Exomiser's HiPhive, PhenIX and Phive prioritisers with INDIGENA's embedding-based best-match-average (BMA) similarity, leaving each prioritiser's gene–phenotype model associations unchanged. Evaluation uses the PAVS benchmark under a disease-disjoint split: Track 1 (phenotype-only, 518 test cases, 2,258-gene pool) and Track 2 (phenotype + variant, 284 cases, 226-gene pool). INDIGENA is trained with TransD on a UPheno-based knowledge graph; the gene→phenotype source (G2) defines three configs (MGI-only, HP-only, MGI+HP). Tables below report the best config per method; full per-config results are in the original report.

Headline. On Track 1, INDIGENA similarity is on par with Exomiser's IC similarity (PhenIX slightly improves). On Track 2, where a variant score is present, INDIGENA substantially improves Mean Rank, Hits@10/100 and AUC across all three prioritisers, at some cost to top-1 (MRR/Hits@1). Phenotype perturbations are largely absorbed once genotype is available (Track 2), while incomplete phenotyping is the most damaging degradation in phenotype-only ranking. In Tables 1–3, bold marks the best value in each column (ties bolded jointly); Table 4 bolds per-track (see its note).

Findings. (1) Incomplete phenotyping is the most damaging perturbation in phenotype-only ranking — restricting to ≤3 terms roughly halves Track 1 MRR for both PhenIX similarities (0.259→0.147; INDIGENA 0.272→0.152, marked red). (2) Genotype buffers phenotype noise: Track 2 (Phive) is nearly flat across all five modes, and INDIGENA-Phive is essentially invariant (MR ≈ 10, AUC ≈ 0.96 throughout) while staying far ahead of Exomiser Phive (MR ≈ 28–32, AUC ≈ 0.86–0.89) under every perturbation. (3) The MGI+HP config collapses under full superclass replacement (Track 1 HiPhive MRR 0.261→0.032; not shown), whereas MGI-only and HP-only generalise gracefully — the combined embedding over-relies on leaf-term neighbourhoods.

Methods (brief)

INDIGENA replaces only the pairwise phenotype-similarity function; gene–phenotype model associations (OMIM/MGI/ZFIN) are unchanged. Knowledge graph: G1 UPheno OWL2Vec* projection (771K triples; HP/MP/UPHENO), G2 gene→phenotype (config-dependent), G3 training-case disease→HP (test/val diseases excluded for inductiveness), G4 training-case gene↔disease. Training: TransD, 100-d embeddings, Adam, batch 2,048, lr 1e-3. Split: disease-disjoint 80/10/10, cohort-stratified; results on the 518-case test split (Track 1) / 284-case leakage-free subset (Track 2). Metrics macro-averaged over cases.
